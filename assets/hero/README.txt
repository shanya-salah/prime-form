OPTIONAL: your own gym photos for the home page background
==========================================================
By default the home page shows the 3D gym that the app draws itself.
If you want real photos instead (for example of your university gym):

1. Put 6 landscape photos here, named exactly:
   slide-1.jpg  slide-2.jpg  slide-3.jpg  slide-4.jpg  slide-5.jpg  slide-6.jpg
   (about 1920 x 1080 pixels, under 500 KB each is ideal)
2. In the website open  Settings -> Appearance -> 3D background -> "My photos".

Only use photos you took yourself or have permission to use.
